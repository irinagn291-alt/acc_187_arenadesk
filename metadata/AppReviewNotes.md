# Arenadesk — App Review Notes

Arenadesk is an offline operations desk for a gaming club or esports venue. The intended audience is venue owners, managers, and floor staff who need shifts, seats, maintenance, tournaments, stock, and cash tools on a single device.

## Access for Review
Launch the app and use the Dashboard immediately. There is no cloud login, account creation, or paywall. Local staff PIN / role gates—if present in seeded data—are on-device only and are not a registration system. All primary features are reachable without external credentials.

Suggested path:

A. Open Dashboard and review shift status, zone readiness, and alerts.  
B. Open or close a shift using the provided flow and checklist steps.  
C. Open Floor and inspect seats / zones and a seat detail health view.  
D. Open Tournaments, view a running or sample event, and inspect bracket/standings UI.  
E. Browse Staff / More for inventory, finance, maintenance, or analytics panels as available.  
F. Confirm export/backup options remain local to the device.

## Advertising, Accounts, and Analytics
The app does not display ads and contains no ad monetization. It does not require user registration or cloud login. Any PIN or role gate is a local venue control, not Sign in with Apple, email auth, or Firebase. There are no analytics, attribution, or advertising SDKs; the Analytics screen reads on-device records only.

## Originality (Guideline 4.3)
Arenadesk targets a specific venue-ops niche: shift checklists with cash close, seat/zone health scoring, maintenance scheduling, tournament brackets with seeding/standings, and local inventory/finance—unified in a console-style UI for one offline floor. It is not a reskin, clone, or repackaged template of another developer’s app.
