## App Store Description
Keep the floor running even when the network does not.

Arenadesk is an on-device operations desk for a gaming club or esports venue. Open and close shifts against versioned checklists, watch zone and seat health, schedule maintenance before hardware fails, run tournaments with seeding and standings, and keep stock and cash records in one place.

Staff roles and local PINs gate sensitive actions on the device—there is no cloud account. Analytics stay local so managers can review the shift without sending data off-site.

FLOOR OPERATIONS IN ONE PLACE
— Open and close shifts with checklists and cash notes
— Monitor zones and seats with readiness and health scores
— Log maintenance and repairs against equipment
— Run tournaments: brackets, seeding, matches, standings
— Track inventory levels and finance records
— Review local analytics for the venue
— Export JSON or CSV backups from the device

Designed for single-venue use offline. No ads, no subscriptions, and no internet connection required.
